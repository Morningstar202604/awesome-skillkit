# Sources & Methodology — article-drafter v2.0: Origins of Drafting Tacit Knowledge

## Evidence discipline

1. Writing methodology is "highly subjective human knowledge"; **AI is forbidden from inventing principles on its own** — every piece of tacit knowledge must have a checkable public source.
2. Every claim is cross-checked against at least two independent sources (original text / reliable secondhand retelling).
3. Single-source, non-cross-verifyable, or suspected-fabricated-precise-number claims are **not included**.
4. When transplanting English writing rules into Chinese, adapt each critically and annotate (e.g. the passive-voice entry), rather than copying one-for-one.

| Meaning before wording; critique of prefabricated phrases | George Orwell, "Politics and the English Language", *Horizon* (1946) (full text verified against the UPenn archive) | Consistent across Open Culture / Marquette Law / EL PAÍS retellings | "Get one's meaning as clear as one can through pictures or sensations" precedes word choice; rules (i)–(vi) checked against the original |

## Critical adaptation for the Chinese context (record of recognition)

| English original rule | Chinese treatment | Rationale |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Orwell rule (iv): Never use the passive | **Not transplanted** as a ban | Chinese patient-voice sentences (bei-construction) are a natural expression ("the file was deleted"); mechanically banning them produces awkward prose; what transplants is the motive "don't use the passive to hide the responsible party" |
| Orwell rule (i): Never use a metaphor you are used to seeing in print | **Transplanted and extended** into a "prefabricated phrases" list | Chinese counterparts: empower/lever/closed loop/go viral/in-depth analysis — same root cause (prefabricated language lets thought slack); the entries are this skill's Chinese mapping from the same principle, not the original canon |
| King "kill adverbs" | **Transplanted** into "cut needless words" | Chinese adverb redundancy (intensifier + de) is isomorphic to English -ly abuse |
