## Distillation boundary (honest disclaimer)

- The nine-type division in the question-type decision table is the author's generalization of common K-12 assignment forms in China, not an official taxonomy; the "other" exit exists to leave room for types outside the boundary.
- The default scoring-dimension table is experiential; a teacher's explicit grading criteria always take priority (the override rule in SKILL.md step 4).
- Bloom's taxonomy is used in this skill only for the single principle of "decidabilization," not a full cognitive-level mapping.
- This skill is a methodology distillation and does not represent the official views of the cited works or project authors.
