# Price Negotiation

The number on the billboard is not the price. The number in writing on the
contract, as the bare-car price, is the price.

## Demand the bare-car price (裸车价)

Dealers advertise 综合优惠 (combined discount) — a big number that bundles the
actual cash discount with worthless add-ons (floor mats, window film, "free"
maintenance packages you would never buy). Always ask for, in writing:

```
裸车价: ¥________
```

That is the price of the car before tax, insurance, and fees. Everything else is
negotiated separately.

- If the dealer refuses to write down the bare-car price, **walk away**. Another
  dealer will.
- Compare at least 2-3 dealers in the same city for the same trim.
- Do not negotiate monthly payments; negotiate the total bare-car price.

## Separate loan vs cash offers

Dealers often quote a lower "cash" price and a higher "loan" price, then build a
hidden dealer markup into the loan. Treat them as two separate questions:

1. What is the bare-car price if I pay cash today?
2. What is the bare-car price if I finance? What is the actual APR, and what
   fees does the dealer add on top?

Compare with an outside bank / credit-union loan offer. Do not let the dealer
roll loan markup into the car price.

## Inventory car (库存车) rules

A car that has been sitting on the lot for months is not a "new car" in the
usual sense — tires age, battery drains, fluids degrade.

- Definition (industry convention):
  - Domestic brand: **more than 3 months** from door-jamb build date to delivery
  - Imported brand: **more than 6 months**
- Check the door-jamb build date (see delivery-inspection.md).
- If it is inventory: negotiate **5-10% off** the already-discounted price,
  demand fresh oil change, new battery check, and confirm tire age is still fine.
- Do not pay new-car money for an inventory car.

## Timing leverage

- **End of quarter / end of year:** sales staff hit monthly and quarterly
  targets. They will discount harder to close the number.
- **End of month, last few days:** strongest leverage.
- **Model-year changeover:** outgoing-year models discount heavily when the new
  model lands.
- **Holiday sales events:** often noise; verify the bare-car price beat the
  pre-event price.

## Deposit (定金) rules

- **Never pay 定金 before price, delivery date, and free items are all written
  in the contract.**
- Distinguish 定金 (deposit, often non-refundable) from 订金 (advance, usually
  refundable). Ask which one you are signing.
- If the contract says non-refundable without conditions, walk away or negotiate
  a refund clause.
- No deposit until you have the bare-car price in writing.

## Negotiation script (compact)

1. "What is the bare-car price (裸车价) on this trim, in writing, today?"
2. "What else is mandatory? I do not want decoration, insurance bundling, or
   maintenance packages."
3. "If I finance through you, what is the APR and what dealer fees are on top?"
4. "What is the build date on the car you would deliver?" (catches inventory)
5. "I will compare across dealers; I will come back when the number is
   competitive." Then actually leave. Silence and leaving work.
