# Maintenance Intervals

The owner's manual (保养手册) is the authority. The 4S service adviser's
recommendations are a sales conversation. Below are general intervals; **when
the manual for your exact model differs, follow the manual.**

## Core interval table

| Item | Interval | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Engine oil + filter (小保养) — full synthetic | **10,000 km OR 1 year**, whichever comes first | most new gas cars use full synthetic |
| Engine oil + filter — semi-synthetic | 7,500 km or 1 year | |
| Engine oil + filter — mineral oil | 5,000 km or 6 months | older / budget cars |
| Engine air filter | 20,000 km (inspect at 10,000 km) | clean in dusty environments sooner |
| Cabin / AC filter | 20,000 km OR 1 year | replace sooner if allergy season / bad smell |
| Brake fluid | **2 years OR 40,000 km** | hygroscopic — absorbs water over time regardless of km |
| Coolant (antifreeze) | per manual, usually 4 years / 80,000 km for long-life | do not flush every 2 years |
| Brake pads | replace when pad material is **< 3 mm** | shops may say 5 mm "to be safe" — that is upsell |
| Brake rotors | replace when below minimum thickness (stamped on rotor) or warped (pulsing brake) | |
| Spark plugs — iridium | 80,000–100,000 km | long-life |
| Spark plugs — copper / nickel | 20,000–30,000 km | |
| Transmission fluid | per manual; many say "lifetime" but 60,000–80,000 km is prudent | "lifetime" usually means lifetime of the fluid under warranty, not lifetime of the car |
| Timing belt | per manual, usually 60,000–100,000 km | if it breaks, engine damage — do not skip |
| Timing chain | "lifetime" per most manuals | listen for rattling on cold start; replace if noisy |
| Tire rotation | every 10,000 km | evens wear |
| Tires | replace when tread depth < 1.6 mm **or** tires older than 5 years | age matters more than tread |
| Wiper blades | every 6–12 months, or when they streak | cheap to DIY |
| 12V battery | 3–5 years typical | test if it struggles to start in winter |

## The 4S shop push list (usually unnecessary)

These are commonly sold at service but are not in the owner's manual unless
you have a specific symptom:

- **Engine flush (发动机清洗)** — unnecessary on schedule; only if oil has
  gone unchanged for a very long time.
- **Fuel system cleaning / injector cleaning (油路清洗 / 喷油嘴清洗)** —
  unnecessary unless you have a confirmed misfire or performance drop.
- **Throttle body cleaning (节气门清洗)** — only when idle is rough or
  check-engine light points to it.
- **Engine internal protection / metal conditioner** — snake oil; modern oils
  already have additives.
- **AC disinfection / odor treatment every service** — do if smell returns, not
  on a fixed schedule.
- **Transmission flush at every service** — follow the manual interval, not the
  service adviser's.

Polite refusal: "Please follow the owner's manual schedule. I will decline
additional services unless the diagnostic shows a specific need."

## EV-specific notes

- EVs have no engine oil, no spark plugs, no timing belt.
- Still service: brake fluid (2 years), coolant for the battery (per manual),
- cabin air filter (same as gas), tires, wipers, 12V battery.
- Battery health check at service intervals; ask for a cell-balance / capacity
  readout.
- Brake pads last much longer (regenerative braking); inspect rather than
  replace on schedule.

## Keeping records

- Save every service invoice in one folder (digital scan is fine).
- Note what was actually done, not just what was recommended.
- Service records are also used-car resale value — a documented maintenance
  history adds price.
