# Contract Red Flags

Read the contract before signing. Verbal promises do not survive delivery day.
Every important point must be in writing.

## Red flags

### 1. Price is not stated as the bare-car price

- The contract should state **裸车价 (bare-car price)** as a specific number,
  not just "total" or "as agreed."
- If the price is vague or bundled into a monthly payment, refuse to sign.
- The total out-the-door price (裸车价 + tax + insurance + fees) should also be
  itemized.

### 2. Mandatory decoration package (装饰包)

- A contract line item like "decoration package ¥8,000" that you did not choose.
- Floor mats, window film, coating, and "premium" accessories are often marked
  up 3-5x.
- Either negotiate it out, or negotiate it down to a fair price you would
  actually pay.

### 3. Vague delivery date

- Contract says "deliver when available" or "estimated Q4."
- Must state a **specific delivery date** and a remedy if the dealer misses it
  (e.g. daily penalty, or full refund of deposit).
- "Car in transit, any day now" with no date = inventory-car problem they are
  hiding.

### 4. Non-refundable deposit (定金) without conditions

- 定金 is legally non-refundable in China. 订金 is usually refundable.
- The contract should specify: refundable if dealer misses delivery date, if
  the car is not as agreed, or if records show issues.
- Never pay a non-refundable deposit before the bare-car price, delivery date,
  and free items are all written.

### 5. No written promise of "free" items

- Sales person verbally promises "free window film, free floor mats, free
  maintenance."
- If it is not on the contract, it does not exist. Write every free item into
  the contract with brand / quantity.

### 6. "Final price" excludes mandatory fees

- The quoted "final price" suddenly excludes 上牌费 (registration fee), 出库费
  (delivery fee), PDI fee, or other add-ons on signing day.
- Require the contract to list **all fees** and state "this is the total
  out-the-door price, no further charges."

### 7. Loan terms not in writing

- If financing: the contract must state the loan amount, APR, term (months),
  monthly payment, total interest, and any early-repayment penalty.
- Verbal "low interest" with no written APR is a trap.
- Watch for hidden dealer markup built into the loan.

### 8. Missing warranty / maintenance book stamp

- The warranty booklet must be stamped by the dealer at delivery.
- Without the stamp, warranty service may be disputed later.

### 9. Used-car "as is" language

- For used cars, a contract that says "sold as-is, no warranty" after the seller
  promised a clean history is a red flag.
- The records (出险记录, 维保记录) should be attached as contract annexes.

## Before you sign

- Read every line, even the small print.
- Take a photo of every page before signing.
- Do not let the sales person rush you.
- If a line is unclear, ask them to write a plain-language explanation on the
  contract itself and initial it.
