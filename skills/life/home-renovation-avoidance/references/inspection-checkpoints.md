# Inspection Checkpoints (分阶段验收)

Stage-by-stage acceptance with numeric thresholds. Do not let the next stage
proceed until the current one passes — especially hidden works (隐蔽工程),
which are covered up and impossible to recheck later. Photograph/video every
stage before it is covered.

## 1. 水电隐蔽验收 (Water & Electric Hidden Works)

- **Water pressure test:** raise system to **>=0.8 MPa and hold for 30 minutes**;
  pressure drop within tolerance, no visible leak at joints.
- **Wire gauge:** verify against the load circuit — lighting loop thinner,
  socket loop >=2.5 sqmm, high-power circuits (AC, water heater) >=4 sqmm.
- **Conduit filling rate:** wires occupy **<=40% of conduit cross-section** so
  they can be pulled/replaced later.
- No sharp conduit bends; junction boxes accessible; hot/neutral/ground correctly
  color-coded.

## 2. 防水验收 (闭水试验 / Closed-Water Test)

- **Duration >=48 hours** of standing water.
- **Inspect the downstairs ceiling** (楼下天花板), not just your own floor — a
  leak only shows up on the neighbor's side.
- **Shower wall waterproof height >=1.8 m** (some cities require 1.8 m; the
  whole wet-room wall is safer).
- Seal at pipe penetrations and floor-wall junctions.

## 3. 瓷砖验收 (Tiling)

- **Tap every tile** with a mallet/coin; hollow sound = hollowing (空鼓).
- **Hollowing rate <=5% overall, and NO hollow tiles on wall edges/corners**
  (edge hollowing causes tiles to fall off).
- Bathroom floor has the correct **gradient (坡度)** toward the drain so water
  does not pool.
- Grout full and even; no lippage (uneven tile edges).

## 4. 木工 / 油漆 (Carpentry & Paint)

- **Board grade: E1 minimum; ENF or E0 for a kids' room.** Demand the official
  test report (检测报告) matching the delivered board batch.
- Paint film even, no sagging (流坠), no brush marks, no color difference.
- Cabinet doors align, hinges adjust, drawers slide.

## 5. 竣工验收 (Final Handover)

- Test **every switch and socket** (socket tester).
- Check **water flow and drainage** at every faucet.
- Doors close/latch properly; windows seal.
- Cabinet hardware all present and working.
- Site cleaned; all promised fittings delivered.
- Walk a written punch-list (整改单) together; do not pay the final amount
  until every item is closed.

## Reference Standards

- GB 50210 — unified standard for construction quality of decoration works.
- GB 50327 — residential decoration engineering规范 / code.
