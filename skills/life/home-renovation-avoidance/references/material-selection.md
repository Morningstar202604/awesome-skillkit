# Material Selection & Delivery Verification (材料验收)

The contract names a product; the delivered product is often a cheaper variant.
Verify on delivery, before installation — once installed, substitution is very
hard to prove.

## What To Check On Delivery

1. **Brand + model + grade on the packaging itself.** Compare the printed box
   label against the contract line item, not against a photo on a website.
2. **Photograph every package.** Shoot the brand logo, model number, production
   batch, and grade stamp before anyone opens it.
3. **Demand the test report (检测报告).** Especially for boards (甲醛
   emission / 环保等级), paint, and wire. The report batch should match the
   delivered batch.
4. **Count quantities** against the order; short counts become paid "extras"
   later.

## Common Substitution Tricks (偷换材料)

| Trick | How It Happens | Defense |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| Tile grade downgrade | A-grade sample shown, B/C-grade (合格品/优等品) delivered | check grade stamp on tile back; refuse on site |
| Board grade mislabel | E0 sample shown, E1/E2 board installed | cross-check report batch; cut a small panel edge |
| Wire gauge thinner | 4 sqmm specified, 2.5 sqmm run | measure conductor diameter; compare to spec |
| Paint series swapped | premium series quoted, standard series delivered | check can series/batch; keep one unopened can |
| Fake / parallel goods | unbranded goods passed as brand | verify via brand official anti-counterfeit channel |

## Photograph / Video Shot List (on delivery)

- Pallet / stack overview showing total quantity.
- Close-up of each label: brand, model, grade, batch, production date.
- The delivered item next to the contract spec line (screenshot).
- Any damage or mismatch, before signature.

**Rule:** do not sign the delivery note until the goods match the contract.
Signing is your acceptance and waives later claims.
