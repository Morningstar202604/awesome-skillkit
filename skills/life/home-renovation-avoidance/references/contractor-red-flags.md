# Contractor Red Flags (装修公司/施工队黑名单特征)

Vet the company before signing. Many disputes trace back to choosing a
fly-by-night operator. Cross-check the business registration; these are
patterns reported by consumer-protection media in 2025-2026.

## Company-Level Red Flags

- **Established less than ~1 year** (新成立公司) — high risk of vanishing
  before the defect period ends.
- **Frequent changes of legal representative (法人频繁变更) or registered
  address** — a common way to escape prior liability.
- **Refusal to put any change in writing** — everything is "we'll take care of
  it" / verbal promises.
- **High-pressure sales** — "sign today", limited-time discounts, pushing you to
  pay a large 定金 on the spot.
- **"Today-only discount" / 限时优惠** urgency tactics to bypass contract review.
- **No physical office** (no storefront you can visit), or a showroom that is
  rented month-to-month.
- Very low quote versus the market average — the classic low-ball that becomes
  add-ons.
- Asking for large private-account transfers instead of the company account.

## Worker-Level Red Flags (individual 施工队)

- No contract at all, or a one-page handwritten note.
- Cannot show a completed local project you can visit.
- Pushes you to buy materials through them "at a discount" (kickback /
  substitution channel).

## What To Ask Before Signing

1. Business license and years in operation; check 企业信用信息公示系统.
2. A real, local completed project you may inspect.
3. Itemized contract with brand+model+grade and add-on cap.
4. Company bank account + invoice (发票) for every payment.
