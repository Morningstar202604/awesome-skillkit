# Payment Discipline (付款节点)

The single highest-leverage protection is **when you release money**. Every
payment should follow a passed inspection, not precede it.

## Milestone Rule of Thumb

| Stage | Suggested Release | Condition |
|-------|-------------------|-----------|
| Contract signing | **<=30% upfront** | never exceed 30%; refuse 50/50 demands |
| Hidden works passed | next tranche | pressure test + waterproofing signed off |
| Main materials / tiling passed | next tranche | no hollow tiles, materials match contract |
| Carpentry & paint passed | next tranche | board grade report, paint film OK |
| Final handover | final balance / retainer | punch-list 整改单 fully closed |

- Keep a **retainer (质保金)** (e.g. 5-10%) for a defect period after handover.
- If a **third-party escrow / platform supervision payment (第三方托管/平台监管)**
  is available, use it — the money is only released on your confirmation.

## Hard Rules

- **Never pay the final amount before the punch-list is complete.**
- **Never pay in cash to a personal account** when an invoice (发票) is due.
- Each payment should reference a passed, signed inspection record.
- If the contractor demands more money mid-job, **stop and demand a written,
  itemized basis** tied to an approved design change — do not pay under pressure.

## If The Contractor Demands More Money (增项加价)

1. Do not argue on site under pressure; say you will review it in writing.
2. Ask for a written change order (变更单) with the item, unit price, quantity,
   and the design change it is based on.
3. Cross-check against the 5% add-on cap in the contract.
4. Only pay what is contractually owed; withhold the disputed amount.
5. If they threaten to stop work, preserve evidence (chat records, photos) and
   escalate to 12315 / local 市场监管.
