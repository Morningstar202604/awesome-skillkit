# Contract Traps (装修合同陷阱)

This reference lists the contract clauses consumers must force into writing
before paying. If a contractor refuses to put it in writing, treat that as a
red flag on its own.

## The Must-Have Written Rules

- **预算即决算，增项不超过 5%** — "The budget is the final settlement price;
  unapproved add-on items (增项) may not exceed 5%." This must appear verbatim in
  the contract, not just in a sales pitch.
- **Every material = brand + model + grade.** No vague entries. "一线品牌"
  (first-tier brand) or "同档次" (same grade) is not acceptable. The contract
  must spell out the exact product model and its grade (e.g. board E0/ENF/E1,
  tile grade, paint series).
- **Refuse 定金 that locks you into a package.** A non-refundable 定金 tied to
  "today only" discounts or bundled packages is a trap. Prefer a small, clearly
  refundable booking fee; never pay a large lock-in deposit under pressure.
- **Payment tied to passed milestones.** Each payment tranche is released only
  after that stage passes acceptance. Never pay for work not yet inspected.

## Common Add-On Scam (增项拆分)

The contractor quotes a low base price, then splits a single task into many
line items so the final bill balloons. Classic example: wall paint is split into

- 墙面找平 (wall leveling)
- 贴绷带 (joint tape)
- 底漆 (primer)
- 面漆 (topcoat)
- 阴阳角处理 (inside/outside corner treatment)
- 成品保护 (finished-surface protection)

A job that should be one per-square-meter line item becomes 5-7 separate charges.
**Defense:** require a unit-price quote per sqm / per linear meter that already
bundles the standard prep work, and state that no extra charge may be added for
prep that a professional painter must already do.

## Other Contract Red Flags

- No itemized bill — only a lump sum.
- "We'll handle the details later" / verbal promises not in the contract.
- No penalty clause for delayed handover or defective rework.
- No scope listing of what IS included, so anything can be charged as extra.
- Asking for cash or private transfer to a personal account instead of the
  company account with an invoice (发票).
