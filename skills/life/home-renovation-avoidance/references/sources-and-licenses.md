# Sources and Licenses (来源与许可)

This skill compiles publicly reported consumer-protection guidance and
national/industry acceptance standards. Numeric thresholds are reference values;
always verify against the latest local notice before acting.

## Consumer-Protection Reporting (2025-2026)

- 新华网 (Xinhua Net) — consumer-rights reporting on renovation scams, add-on
  inflation, and material substitution.
- 人民网 (People's Daily Online) — 315 / consumer-rights themed reporting on
  home-renovation disputes.
- 央视网 (CCTV.com) — consumer exposure programs (e.g. 315晚会-related reporting)
  on contractor traps and rent/renovation scams.
- Local Consumers Association (各地消协 / 消费者协会) published warnings and
  model contract clauses.
- 12315 market-regulation complaint channel (国家市场监督管理总局).

## Acceptance Standards

- **GB 50210** — Unified Standard for Construction Quality Decoration
  (建筑装饰装修工程质量验收标准).
- **GB 50327** — Code for Residential Decoration Engineering (住宅装饰装修工程
  施工规范).
- Indoor air / formaldehyde board grades: E1, E0, and the newer **ENF** tier
  (lowest emission) per current GB indoor-decorating material standards.

## Enforcement / Help Channels

- 消费者协会 (Consumers Association) — mediation of consumer disputes.
- 12315 — market-regulation complaint and reporting hotline / platform.
- 12345 — general government service hotline for routing complaints.
