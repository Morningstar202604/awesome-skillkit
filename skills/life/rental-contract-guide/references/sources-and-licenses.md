# Sources and Licenses (来源与许可)

This skill compiles publicly available PRC rental law and consumer-protection
guidance. It is legal information, not legal advice; consult a lawyer or the
12348 hotline for live disputes.

## Primary Legal Sources

- **Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (中华人民共和国民法典):**
  - **Article 705** — the lease term may not exceed 20 years; beyond 20 years the
    excess is invalid.
  - **Articles 586-588** — earnest money (定金): the parties may agree on earnest
    money as security; it is capped at **20% of the subject-matter amount** (for
    a lease, the annual rent); the paying party's breach forfeits it, the
    receiving party's breach doubles it.
- **住建部 / housing-authority rental regulations** — urban housing leasing
  administration rules, including requirements on registration, illegal
  partitions (隔断房), and landlord/tenant duties.

## Help / Complaint Channels

- **12348** — public legal-aid hotline (法律咨询 / 法律援助).
- **12345** — general government service hotline for routing rental complaints.
- **12378** — financial consumer hotline, relevant for rent-loan (租金贷)
  disputes.
- Local consumer association (消费者协会) for consumer-side mediation.

## Consumer-Protection Reporting

- 新华网 / 人民网 / 央视网 2025-2026 reporting on rental scams: fake landlords,
  rent-loan (租金贷) traps, sub-landlord (二房东) evictions, and illegal
  partitions (隔断房).
