# Deposit Rules (定金 / 订金 / 押金)

The three Chinese terms are legally different; confusing them is a top cause of
lost money.

## 定金 vs 订金 vs 押金

| Term | Meaning | Refundable? | Cap / Note |
|------|---------|-------------|------------|
| 定金 (earnest money) | binds the deal; a penalty instrument | **Non-refundable if YOU back out; doubled back if the OWNER backs out** | Capped at **20% of the annual rent** under the Civil Code; anything above 20% is not protected as 定金 |
| 订金 (prepayment / goodwill money) | advance payment, no penalty | **Refundable** if no deal | no penalty; must be returned |
| 押金 (security deposit) | collateral for damages/rent | Refundable at end, minus lawful deductions | typically 1-2 months rent |

**Rule of thumb for tenants:** when pressured to pay "定金" on the spot, pause.
Understand it is non-refundable for you and capped at 20% of annual rent. Prefer
the refundable 订金 wording if you are still deciding.

## What May / May Not Be Deducted

- **Normal wear (正常损耗) is NOT deductible:** light wall scuffs, normal floor
  wear, aging paint, worn-out seals from everyday use.
- **The landlord must provide evidence of damage:** itemized list, photos, and
  repair receipts. A vague "the place is dirty / damaged" claim is not enough.
- Deductions should be for **actual damage beyond normal wear** (a hole in the
  wall, a broken appliance, a stained carpet you caused).

## Common Deposit Deduction Scams

- Withholding the whole deposit over minor cleaning, then "keeping the rest".
- Charging for painting the whole flat for one small scuff.
- Refusing to return the deposit unless you "find the next tenant".
- Claiming pre-existing defects you photographed at move-in were caused by you.

## Protection

- Do the joint move-in walkthrough (see `move-in-walkthrough.md`) and list every
  existing defect with photos.
- Do the joint move-out walkthrough together and get a **written deposit return
  confirmation** (see `move-out-checklist.md`).
