# Identity Verification (身份核验)

Before paying anything, confirm who actually owns the unit and whether the
person in front of you has the right to rent it to you.

## The Owner (房东) Case

- See the **房产证 /不动产权证** (property ownership certificate) AND the
  **房东 ID card** (房东身份证). The name on the certificate should match the
  ID.
- Ideally verify the property ownership status directly at the **不动产登记
  中心** (real-estate registration center) or via its official online query,
  especially for large deposits.
- Pay rent and deposit only to a name matching the owner on the certificate.

## The Sub-Landlord (二房东) Case

If the person renting to you is not the registered owner:

- See the **original lease** between the sub-landlord and the owner.
- Confirm the original lease **explicitly allows subletting (转租)**, and that
  the sublet term does not exceed the remaining original lease term.
- Ideally **all three parties meet** (owner, sub-landlord, you) and the owner
  confirms in writing.
- Without written sublet permission, the owner can later evict you even if you
  paid.

## Fake-Landlord Patterns (二房东/假房东套路)

Refuse and walk away if you hear:

- "I forgot the contract / certificate at home."
- "The owner is my relative / parent, I can rent it out."
- Pressure to pay immediately: "the unit will be gone by tonight."
- Cannot introduce you to the registered owner at all.
- Wants payment to a personal account with no invoice / receipt.

**Rule:** never transfer money until identity and ownership are verified. A
verified ID + certificate (and, for sub-landlords, the original lease with
sublet permission) is the entry condition.
