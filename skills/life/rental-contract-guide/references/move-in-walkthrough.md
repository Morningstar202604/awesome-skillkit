# Move-In Walkthrough (入住交接)

Document the condition at move-in so you cannot be billed for it at move-out.
Do this **before you receive keys / pay the balance**, and sign it together.

## Photograph / Video Everything

- Walk every room; shoot wide video of each room and close-ups of:
  - every **stain, crack, hole, scratch, chip** on walls, floors, doors, windows.
  - every **appliance** (fridge, washer, AC, water heater, stove, hood) with
    power on / off, showing it works.
  - every **faucet, shower, drain**, running water.
  - every **switch and socket**, every **lock**, every **window seal**.
- Send the dated photos/video to the landlord in chat as written acknowledgment.

## Record Utility Meter Readings

- Read and photograph **water, electric, and gas meters** at move-in.
- Agree in writing that bills before the reading are the landlord's / prior
  tenant's responsibility.

## Test Before Signing

- All appliances turn on and work; AC cools/heats; water heats; drains flow.
- All switches/sockets live; locks lock; windows close and seal.
- No leaks under sinks / behind toilet; no damp/mold smell.

## Sign The Joint Handover List (房屋交接清单)

- A written list of known defects and the **repair responsibility + deadline**
  for each (e.g. "leaking tap, landlord fixes within 3 days").
- Both parties sign; keep a copy. This list is your defense against move-out
  deductions for pre-existing damage.
