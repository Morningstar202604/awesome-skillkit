# Contract Red Flags (合同必备条款与风险)

A verbal "trust me" lease is a future dispute. Every term below should be in
writing and signed by both parties.

## Must-Have Clauses

- **Rent amount** and **payment date / cycle** (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
- **Deposit amount** and the **conditions + timeline for return**.
- **Lease term** (start and end date; remember the 20-year Civil Code ceiling).
- **Repair responsibility** — what the landlord fixes, what the tenant fixes.
- **Rent increase rules** (when, by how much, or fixed for the term).
- **Early termination penalty** for either side.
- **Utility responsibility** — water, electric, gas, network, property
  management fee, who pays.

## Red Flags To Reject

- Vague rent increase like "adjust to market rate" with no cap or notice period.
- **No timeline for returning the deposit** at move-out.
- Landlord may **enter the unit without notice** (privacy breach).
- **All maintenance is the tenant's responsibility** even for plumbing/wiring
  defects that pre-exist.
- One-sided penalty: tenant pays heavy penalty to leave, but landlord can
  terminate freely.
- **租金贷 (rent-loan):** a "monthly rent" plan that actually routes your money
  into a consumer loan / installment product. If the onboarding pushes a loan
  app, credit check, or "rent financing", refuse — you would owe the bank even
  after moving out.
