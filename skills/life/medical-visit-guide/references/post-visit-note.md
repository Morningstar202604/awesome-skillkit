# Post-Visit Note Template

Fill this out **within 10 minutes of leaving the consultation room**, while the
conversation is still fresh. Memory of medical instructions fades fast.

Bring a printed copy or open this template on your phone before the visit.

## How to use this note

1. **During the visit:** write key words as the doctor talks. Do not try to
   write full sentences.
2. **Right after the visit:** sit in the hospital lobby and fill in the blanks
   while memory is fresh.
3. **Before taking a new medicine:** re-read the side effects and the
   "emergency symptoms" line.
4. **At the next visit:** bring this note (and prior notes) so the doctor can
   see the timeline.
5. **If anything is unclear:** call the hospital's consultation / nurse line
   (门诊咨询电话) rather than guessing at dosing.
