# Question List Template

Bring a written list. In a 5-10 minute slot, you will forget half of what you
wanted to ask the moment the doctor walks in.

## The 5 Mandatory Questions (every visit)

Ask these in plain language. Write the doctor's answer on the post-visit note.

1. **What is this?** In plain language, what is the diagnosis or working
   explanation? (Ask for the term written down if you cannot remember it.)
2. **Why this treatment?** What is the recommended treatment, and what are the
   alternatives (including "do nothing and watch")?
3. **How long, and what side effects?** How long will treatment last? What side
   effects should I watch for, and when should I worry?
4. **How do we know it is working?** What should I monitor at home? What tests
   will we repeat, and when? What number or result counts as "improved"?
5. **What if it does not help?** If symptoms do not improve (or get worse),
   when should I come back? What symptoms mean I should come sooner?

## Scenario-specific additions

### Chronic disease (diabetes, hypertension, asthma, etc.)

- What are my target numbers (blood pressure, blood sugar, etc.) at home?
- How often should I measure, and when should I call you?
- Are my current meds still right, or do they need adjustment?
- What lifestyle changes actually move the needle?

### About a new medication

- Do I take this with food, on an empty stomach, morning or evening?
- What if I miss a dose — double up, skip it, or what?
- Does it interact with my other medicines or with food (e.g. grapefruit)?
- How long until I feel a difference?
- Do I need follow-up blood tests while on this?

### About ordered tests (blood work, imaging, etc.)

- Do I need to fast before this test? For how long?
- Any preparation (stop a medicine, hold food, drink water)?
- When and how do I get the results?
- Do I need to book another appointment to review results, or can I call in?

### Follow-up visits

- When should I come back for the next appointment?
- What symptoms mean I should come back sooner than scheduled?
- Is this condition likely to resolve, manage long-term, or need monitoring
  forever?
- Do I need a referral to another specialist?

### For surgery or procedures

- What exactly will be done, and what are the risks?
- What is the recovery time and what can I / cannot do afterward?
- What anesthesia or sedation is used?
- How long is the hospital stay, and how do I prepare at home?

## Delivery tip

Number the list. Tick off each question as it is answered. If the doctor runs
out of time before the list is done, point at the unchecked numbers and say:
"Doctor, I still have a few questions — can I ask the nurse, or should I book a
follow-up?" Do not leave with unanswered critical questions about medication or
symptoms to watch for.
