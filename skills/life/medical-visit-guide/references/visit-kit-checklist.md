# Visit Kit Checklist

Pack the night before. A 5-10 minute consultation rewards preparation; fumbling
for records burns the slot.

## Identity and payment

- [ ] 身份证 (national ID card)
- [ ] 医保卡 / 医保电子凭证 (medical insurance card / electronic voucher)
- [ ] Bank card or phone payment (WeChat / Alipay) for fees not covered by insurance
- [ ] Cash small bills, just in case

## Medical records (in chronological order, newest on top)

- [ ] Prior outpatient records (门诊病历本 / electronic records printed)
- [ ] Prior discharge summaries (出院小结) from hospitalizations
- [ ] Lab results: blood work, urine, imaging reports — newest first
- [ ] **CT / MRI / X-ray films, not just the reports.** Bring the actual film or
      CD. The doctor wants to look at the images, not read a written summary.
- [ ] Prior pathology / biopsy reports if any
- [ ] Vaccination record if relevant

## Medication list (written down)

Write every substance you currently take, even if you think it is irrelevant:

- Drug name (generic name, not just brand)
- Dose (e.g. 20 mg)
- Frequency (once daily, twice daily, etc.)
- How long you have been taking it
- **Include OTC drugs** (painkillers, antacids, vitamins)
- **Include 保健品 / dietary supplements** (fish oil, probiotics, protein
      powder, traditional herbs) — these interact with prescriptions and the
      doctor needs to know

Example:

```
- Amlodipine 5 mg, once daily morning, 3 years
- Metformin 500 mg, twice daily with meals, 2 years
- Vitamin D3 1000 IU, daily (OTC)
- Fish oil 1000 mg, daily (supplement)
```

## Allergies

- [ ] Drug allergies (name the drug and the reaction: rash, breathing issue, etc.)
- [ ] Food allergies
- [ ] Prior bad reactions to anesthesia / contrast dye

## Symptom summary

- [ ] The one-line symptom anchor written down (see symptom-anchor-formula.md)
- [ ] A short timeline: when it started, what changed, what you already tried

## Tools

- [ ] Pen and paper, or phone notes app
- [ ] Phone voice recorder — **ask the doctor's permission before recording**
- [ ] Glasses, hearing aids, magnifier if you need them
- [ ] A companion if symptoms make it hard to walk or concentrate

## Logistics

- [ ] Appointment time and hospital location (check the 挂号 confirmation)
- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early for registration / check-in
- [ ] If fasting tests are expected (blood draw, ultrasound), do not eat or
      drink per the appointment notice
