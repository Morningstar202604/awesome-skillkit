# Emergency Red Flags — Call 120

This list is the **stop gate** for this skill. If any item below describes what
is happening **right now**, do NOT use this skill to prepare for an outpatient
visit. Call 120 immediately or go straight to the nearest emergency department
(急诊).

## Red flags (call 120 now)

| Red flag | Why urgent |
|----------|------------|
| Chest pain, pressure, or tightness lasting more than a few minutes, or radiating to arm/jaw/back | possible heart attack |
| Sudden weakness or numbness on one side of face / arm / leg | possible stroke |
| Slurred speech, trouble speaking or understanding | possible stroke |
| Sudden severe headache ("worst of my life") | possible bleeding in the brain |
| Difficulty breathing, shortness of breath at rest, blue lips | respiratory / cardiac emergency |
| Heavy bleeding that does not stop with pressure | hemorrhage |
| High fever with stiff neck, confusion, or rash that does not fade under a glass | possible meningitis |
| Suicidal thoughts, self-harm urges, feeling unable to stay safe | call 120 / crisis line; do not wait |
| Severe allergic reaction: throat closing, swelling of tongue/lips, widespread hives, faintness | anaphylaxis |
| Pregnancy-related vaginal bleeding, abdominal pain, or sudden severe swelling | obstetric emergency |
| Unconsciousness, fainting, unresponsiveness | any cause |
| Severe abdominal pain that is constant and worsening | appendicitis, ectopic pregnancy, perforation |
| Coughing or vomiting blood | internal bleeding |

## What to do when a red flag is present

1. **Call 120 first.** Do not drive yourself if symptoms are severe.
2. Tell the dispatcher: what happened, when it started, your address, and any
   existing conditions.
3. Unlock the door if possible. Have someone stay with the patient.
4. Do not eat, drink, or take any new medicine while waiting, in case surgery
   is needed.
5. Bring the medication list and ID to the hospital when the ambulance arrives.

## Important boundary

This list is a **consumer safety screen**, not a medical triage protocol. When
in doubt, call 120. It is always better to be assessed in 急诊 and sent home
than to wait at home with a time-sensitive emergency.

**This skill (medical-visit-guide) is for non-emergency outpatient preparation
only. If a red flag is present, stop using this skill and call 120.**
