# Symptom Anchor Formula

The first sentence you say to the doctor sets the tone of the whole 5-10 minute
consultation. Lead with a one-line anchor, then let the doctor ask follow-ups.

## Formula

```
医生，我[时间]前开始[部位][症状]，伴[伴随症状]，有[病史]。
```

English gloss: "Doctor, I started having [symptom] in [body part] [time] ago,
with [associated symptoms], and I have [relevant history]."

That is it. Do not add backstory, do not name a disease. Let the doctor ask.

## Examples

| Symptom | Good anchor (say this) | Bad anchor (do not say) |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| Headache | "Doctor, I've had a right-sided headache for 3 days, worse in the morning, with nausea." | "Doctor, I think I have migraine." |
| Abdominal pain | "Doctor, I've had a burning feeling in my upper abdomen for 2 weeks, worse after meals." | "Doctor, I should have gastritis." |
| Cough | "Doctor, I've had a dry cough for 10 days, worse at night, no fever." | "Doctor, I think I have COVID again." |
| Fever | "Doctor, I've had a fever of 38.5 for 3 days, with sore throat." | "Doctor, I need antibiotics." |
| Chest discomfort | "Doctor, I've had a tight feeling in my chest on and off for a week, worse when I walk fast." | "Doctor, is it my heart?" (this one needs emergency triage first) |
| Back pain | "Doctor, I've had lower back pain for 1 month, worse after sitting, no leg numbness." | "Doctor, my disc is herniated." |
| Fatigue | "Doctor, I've felt tired for 2 months, even after sleep, and I've lost 3 kg." | "Doctor, I must be anemic." |

## What NOT to say

- **Self-diagnosis:** "I think I have gastritis / migraine / the flu." You are
  not the doctor; naming a disease biases the clinician and wastes the slot.
- **Describing a disease instead of a sensation:** say "burning after meals,"
  not "stomach inflammation."
- **Leading with a story:** "Yesterday I went to dinner, then it started..."
  The doctor needs the anchor first; the story comes when asked.
- **Asking for a drug by name:** "Can I get amoxicillin?" Let the doctor decide.
- **Downplaying:** "It's nothing serious, I just..." Do not filter; report facts.

## Common symptom-description mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Vague: "I feel bad" | name the body part and the sensation (burning, dull, sharp, pressure) |
| No time anchor: "It hurts sometimes" | say how long, how often, when it started |
| No aggravating factor: skip it | add what makes it worse or better (food, movement, time of day) |
| Forgetting history | mention chronic conditions and current meds in one breath |
| Bringing a long monologue | stop after the anchor; answer the doctor's questions directly |

Write the anchor on paper or in your phone before the visit. Read it aloud if
nervous.
