# Sources and Licenses

## Scope of these references

The Chinese phrases in this skill are **synthesized from general cultural
knowledge** of mainland Chinese social and workplace communication. They are
illustrative templates the user adapts to their own facts — not copied verbatim
from any single copyrighted source.

## Primary research / evidence

- **arXiv:2602.03107** — *Chinese Mock Politeness* benchmark. Confirms that
  Chinese-language models misclassify pseudo-politeness and high-context
  refusals, which is the core blind spot this skill targets.
- General summaries of Chinese social practice as discussed on public consumer
  platforms (Douyin / 抖音, Sohu / 搜狐, wedding-planning communities such as
  婚礼纪) for gift-money norms and banquet etiquette.
- Consumer-protection reporting on the line between normal gift etiquette and
  commercial bribery, used to define the compliance boundary in
  `social-etiquette.md`.
