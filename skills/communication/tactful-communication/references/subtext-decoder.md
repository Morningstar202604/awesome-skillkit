# Subtext Decoder — Literal to Intent

Chinese high-context speech often says the soft thing while meaning the hard
thing. This table maps common utterances to their *likely* intent. Treat the
right column as a prior, not a fact — always weigh context (who speaks, where,
and whether action follows the words).

## Literal → Intent table

| Literal phrase | Likely intent | Do not hear it as |
|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| 我们再考虑考虑 | Politely declining / a soft "no" | a real maybe that will come back |
| 原则上可以 | Not actually approved; there are unstated conditions | a green light |
| 我尽量 / 我看看 | Likely will not happen | a commitment |
| 再说吧 / 到时候看 | No commitment; drop the thread | an open plan to revisit |
| 你人很好 | Friend-zoned / soft romantic rejection | a compliment that means interest |
| 我不是那个意思 | Don't push this point further | an invitation to keep debating |
| 哈哈哈哈 (from a senior/boss) | Awkwardness or mild displeasure; the topic landed wrong | genuine laughter |
| 有空一起吃饭 | Polite sign-off; not a real invitation | a real plan to make a reservation |
| 我回去跟家人商量一下 | Likely a "no" (a face-saving way to decline) | a genuine pending decision |
| 你提的意见很有建设性 | The idea is shelved; thanks and move on | that it will be adopted |
| 不着急，你慢慢弄 | There *is* an implicit expectation; check it directly | literally no deadline |

## Context cues that override the table

- **Who speaks:** a subordinate's 我尽量 is weaker than a peer's; a boss's 再考虑考虑 is near-final.
- **Venue:** said in a public meeting ≈ for the record; said privately ≈ the real signal.
- **Action follows words:** did a meeting actually get scheduled? did money move? Words without follow-through = the "no" column.
- **Channel:** a curt WeChat 哈哈 vs. a face-to-face smile carry opposite weight.

## Rules of thumb

- **Silence ≠ agreement.** In a meeting, quiet listeners are not nodding along; they may be unconvinced or waiting. Ask explicitly.
- **Modesty ≠ lack of confidence.** 献丑了 / 我随便做做 usually precedes real, polished work. Do not infer weakness.
- **Indirect ≠ maybe.** When the answer is softened (考虑考虑 / 原则上 / 再说吧), read it as a default "no" unless concrete next steps follow.
- **Match the register back:** if someone speaks indirectly to you, they usually expect you to read the room and not force a direct yes/no.
