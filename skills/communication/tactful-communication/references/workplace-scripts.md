# Workplace Scripts

Chinese workplace communication favors the bottom line up front, deference in
public, and written follow-through. Below are reusable scripts.

## 1. Three-part report (三段式汇报)

**Formula:** conclusion in one sentence → evidence / data → decision request.
Busy leaders decide on the conclusion; evidence is there to back it, not to bury
it.

- *Example:*
  「结论：这个季度的活动建议砍掉 B 渠道，预算全压 A。依据是 A 的转化是 B 的三倍、成本只有一半。需要您拍板的是：这周内是否按这个方案调整预算？」
  *Gloss:* "Conclusion: this quarter I suggest cutting channel B and putting the full budget on A. Evidence: A converts at 3x B at half the cost. What I need from you: approve this budget shift this week or not?"

- *Anti-pattern (AI default):* 「关于这个季度，我们做了一些分析，首先看了渠道，然后对比了成本……」 — buries the ask, forces the leader to extract it.

## 2. Pushback framing

Never say 做不了 / 时间不够 / 人手不够 on their own. Frame as resource reality +
a concrete trade-off + let the leader set priority.

- *Example:*
  「按现有资源，如果 C 和 A、B 三个全推进，质量和进度都有风险。我建议先聚焦 A 和 C，B 往后排。优先级请您来定，我按您的排期走。」
  *Gloss:* "With current resources, pushing C, A, and B all at once risks both quality and schedule. I suggest focusing on A and C and moving B later. Please set the priority and I'll execute to that order."

## 3. Meeting rules

- **Speak in the first 3 minutes.** The real decisions are made early; waiting to the end reads as unprepared or disengaged.
- **Never contradict the boss in public.** If you disagree, pull them aside one-on-one after. Public correction costs face for everyone.
- **Two bosses?** Maintain a shared priority document so conflicting instructions surface as a written trade-off rather than a you-are-the-meat-sandwich situation.
- **Follow up in writing:** after any verbal decision, send a short 「同步一下刚才的结论：1)… 2)…」 so it is on record.

## 4. Declining extra work from the boss

**Formula:** name current priorities → state the overload honestly → ask the
boss to re-prioritize (do not just refuse).

- *Example:*
  「领导，我目前手上有 A 和 B 两个优先级任务。如果接 C 的话，需要您帮我排一下优先级，避免三个都做不好。我这边随时按您定的顺序推进。」
  *Gloss:* "Boss, I currently have A and B as priority tasks. If I take on C, could you help me re-prioritize so I don't end up doing all three poorly? I'll proceed in whatever order you set."

- *Why this works:* it is not a refusal; it hands the trade-off up the chain where prioritization actually belongs, while showing the user is committed to delivery.

## 5. Managing up (向上管理)

The rule flips depending on how secure the boss's own power is:

- **Bigger, more secure boss → be relaxed and normal.** People with real, stable
  authority are not threatened by a calm, competent presence. Over-deference to
  them reads as either insecure or sycophantic. Be professional, direct, and
  ordinary.
- **Smaller, less secure boss → gently inflate their authority.** Their power is
  limited, and the part of it they *can* use, they will use on you. Give them
  public face, visible deference, and a sense of control; it costs you little and
  buys you cover.

- *Example (secure big boss, relaxed):*
  「这个方案我觉得有两个走法，各自的利弊我列了一下。我个人倾向 A，不过您怎么看？」
  *Gloss:* "I see two ways to run this, and I've laid out the pros and cons of each. My lean is A — but what's your read?"
  *(Note: a real boss wants your actual judgment, not a yes.)*

- *Example (insecure small boss, inflate their authority):*
  「这块我按您上次说的方向推进了，关键节点我会单独跟您同步，最后怎么拍板还是您来定。」
  *Gloss:* "I've moved this forward in the direction you set last time, and I'll sync key milestones with you one-on-one; the final call stays with you."
  *(Note: signals control and ownership without actually ceding substance.)*

## 6. Dual-manager conflict (两个领导)

When two bosses give conflicting instructions, do not become the meat sandwich who
relays one's complaints to the other.

- **Maintain a shared priority document.** One live doc where both bosses can see
  current priorities and what the other has already decided. Conflicting asks
  surface as a written trade-off in the doc, not as you carrying messages between
  them.
- **Never badmouth one boss to the other.** The information always travels; the
  person who hears the gossip learns you will gossip about them too.
- *Example:*
  「两位领导，我在共享文档里同步一下当前优先级：A 领导那边定了 X，B 领导这边要 Y。两件事这周资源排不开，麻烦两位对齐一下哪个先，我按对齐后的结果执行。」
  *Gloss:* "Both leads, I've synced current priorities in the shared doc: lead A has set X, lead B needs Y. I can't resource both this week — could you two align on which goes first, and I'll execute to whatever you settle on?"

## 7. Meeting timing and in-meeting rules

- **Speak in the first 3 minutes.** People who speak early are perceived as
  roughly 50% more confident and competent; the real decisions are set early, and
  waiting to the end reads as unprepared.
- **Do not raise a big objection right after a decision is made.** It lands as
  obstruction and costs the room face. Park it, then raise it one-on-one or in the
  follow-up write-up.
- **大小领导同时在场学会闭嘴** — when both the senior boss and your direct boss
  are in the room, know when to stay quiet. Answer what is asked; do not volunteer
  opinions that your direct boss has not had a chance to own first.
- *Example of a clean early contribution:*
  「我先说下结论：这个事我建议按 X 走，依据是 Y。细节我后面补。」
  *Gloss:* "Let me lead with the conclusion: I suggest going with X, based on Y. I'll fill in the details after."

## 8. Reporting bad news (汇报坏消息)

Do not hide or smooth over a problem until it explodes. Lead with the impact, what
you have already done, and the options. Hiding bad news is the one reporting failure
that is almost unforgivable.

**Formula (one line):** 「问题已经发生，影响是 X，我已经做了 Y，建议下一步 Z。」
("The problem has occurred, the impact is X, I've already done Y, and I suggest
next step Z.")

- *Example:*
  「跟您同步一个问题：上线后支付接口报错率升到了 8%，影响了大概三百笔订单。我已经先切到了备用渠道止血，现在在查根因。下一步建议：一是先维持备用渠道，二是给受影响用户发补偿，三是今天下班前给您一份根因和修复计划。您看这个节奏可以吗？」
  *Gloss:* "Quick sync on an issue: after launch the payment error rate jumped to 8%, affecting roughly 300 orders. I've already switched to the backup channel to stop the bleeding and I'm tracing the root cause. Next I suggest: keep the backup channel on, send affected users compensation, and get you a root-cause and fix plan by end of day. Does that pace work for you?"

- *Anti-pattern (AI default / what to avoid):* going dark, then surfacing a small
  problem as a big one after customers notice; or burying the bad news at the end
  of a long update after a paragraph of good news.
- *Why this works:* bad news delivered fast, already triaged, with options, reads as
  ownership. The same news delivered late, with no action taken, reads as panic and
  negligence.
