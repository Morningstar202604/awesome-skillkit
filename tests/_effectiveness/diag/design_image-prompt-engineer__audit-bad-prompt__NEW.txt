### Audit Report

**Diagnosis: Critical**
This prompt is the textbook example of "keyword shouting" (2023-era style) combined with "empty adjectives" and command-style negatives. It tells the model *what you want it to feel*, but not *what the image actually looks like*.

**Checklist:**
| Segment | Status | Issue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas** | ❌ Miss | No aspect ratio or resolution specified. |
| **Subject** | ❌ Miss | "A woman" is too vague. No hair, clothes, age, or pose details. |
| **Composition** | ❌ Miss | No camera angle, framing, or depth of field. |
| **Style Anchor** | ⚠️ Partial | "Professional photography" is an empty claim. |
| **Text** | ✅ Pass | Explicitly negative (`no text`), though syntax is wrong. |
| **Negatives** | ❌ Fail | Command-style ("no blurry") renders as positive concepts. |
| **Quality Words** | ❌ Fail | `beautiful`, `stunning`, `masterpiece`, `8K`, `super HD` are all weight-diluting noise. |

**Key Fixes Needed:**
1.  **Remove Subjective Adjectives:** Delete `美丽的`, `震撼的`, `最好看`, `最美`, `最棒`. They carry zero visual data.
2.  **Fix Negatives:** Change `no blurry` to `sharp focus` (positive instruction). Change `no bad hands` to `anatomically correct hands` (only if specific; otherwise omit).
3.  **Add Visual Concrete Details:** Replace "drinking coffee" with *how* (holding cup, steam, gaze). Replace "professional photography" with specific gear/lens settings.
